# Make the Firewall requirement and grant caps configurable

No NIP-5D or NAP text sets either cap; both are host policy (#336). Based on staging after #512.

## Tasks
1. Firewall: `maxRequirements`/`maxGrants` on PermissionPolicyOptions, defaults 128/2048,
   validated by `permissionLimits()` against ceilings 1024/16384.
2. Runtime: PermissionAdapter accepts both; coordinator validates at construction; services
   read `maxPermissionRequirements`; a full grant table returns `capacity`, relay reports
   `approval capacity reached`.
3. Services: OUTBOX and COUNT budgets follow the configured bound; router `maxRelayOrigins`;
   OUTBOX maps `capacity` to `approval capacity reached`.
4. Paja: `firewall.maxRequirements`, `firewall.maxGrants` settings in the Firewall group.
5. Tests, docs, changeset, gates, Playwright, PR into staging.

## Left for #513
Other domains (CVM, COUNT, DM, lists, fs, identity, ...) still map `capacity` to their denial
code. The table is still global with no eviction or per-napplet budget, and default-allow
still materializes one grant per origin.
