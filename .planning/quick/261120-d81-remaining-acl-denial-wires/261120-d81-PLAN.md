# Deny keys, media, config, outbox.resolveRelays and upload info/status on their NAP wires (kehto/web#481)

Scope: `catalog/acl-denial` in `kehto conformance --profile full` failed on staging
(`06e841c3`) for keys, media, outbox, config and upload. Their denials fell
through `policy-denials.ts` to the generic `<type>.error`, which no NAP defines.

## Refs (re-resolved 2026-11-20, all heads unchanged from #463)

- NAP-KEYS #9 `8869a4cf`, NAP-MEDIA #10 `7806f438` (+ #133 `8ea49c33`, stacked on

## Decisions

- Defined failure shapes: `keys.registerAction.result` (+`actionId`, `error`),
  `media.session.create.result` (`error`), `config.registerSchema.result`
  (`ok: false`, `error`).
- Fire-and-forget requests are explicit silent rows.
- Spec gaps (bounded, documented in `policy-denials.ts` and RUNTIME-SPEC.md):
  `actionId` echo; no `code` on the config ACK; silent `config.get`; empty
  policy plan for `resolveRelays`; error-only `upload.info`/`upload.status`.
- MEDIA: #10 cited; #133 is not competing.
- Removed the services tests that re-implemented the old `<type>.error`
  composition locally; the real path is covered in `policy-denials.test.ts`.
